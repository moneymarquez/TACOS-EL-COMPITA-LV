Our story

Started in Vegas. Parked in Sandy. Steak cut every morning.

El Compita began as a single stand in Las Vegas. The truck now runs in Sandy, Utah, and a lot of the regulars followed it here. The steak isn't pre-cut or frozen — it's broken down by hand each morning before the grill comes on, and it's why the asada tastes the way it does.

“One taco stand in every city.”
